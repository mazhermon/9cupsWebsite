# DJ 9Cups — Colour Palette Reference

## Core Palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Violet | `#3B1A6E` | Primary dark — depth, mystery |
| Electric Amethyst | `#8B3AC4` | Primary mid — energy, identity |
| Bright Lavender | `#C47EE8` | Primary light — air, celebration |
| Lilac Mist | `#D4AAEE` | Highlight/pale — softness |
| Crimson Rose | `#B03060` | Accent warm — passion, depth |
| Hot Magenta | `#CC2E90` | Accent vivid — urgency, heat |
| Dark Forest | `#1A2A1E` | Ground dark — anchor |
| Warm Asphalt | `#3A3530` | Neutral ground |
| Bone White | `#F0EBE0` | Negative space |
| Acid Moss | `#7A8A1A` | Occasional organic accent |

## Proportional Use
- 60% Purple family (Deep Violet → Lilac Mist)
- 20% Warm accent family (Crimson Rose → Hot Magenta)
- 15% Neutral grounds (Dark Forest, Warm Asphalt, Bone White)
- 5% Acid Moss — sparingly, for organic grounding

## Emotional Temperature by Purple
- Cool blue-violet → introspection, depth
- Electric saturated purple → celebration, live energy
- Warm magenta-purple → love, connection
