# DJ 9Cups — Brand Reference Card
*Pin this to the Project. This is the quick-reference version of the full brand philosophy.*

---

## Who
**DJ 9Cups** — Wellington-based DJ/producer. Named after the Nine of Cups tarot card (The Wish Card): emotional fulfilment, quiet abundance, presence.

## Archetype
**The Wise Enjoyer.** Not seeking, not striving. Already here, already satisfied, sharing generously. Arms crossed, nine cups arranged behind them, smiling.

## Visual Signature
**Double-exposure photographic layering.** Every piece of art overlays real photography with colour washes, abstract fields, or other images. The seams are visible — intentionally. Reality has more in it than first appears.

## Typography Direction
- **Wordmark / headings**: Old-style serif — round, unhurried, slightly archaic. Literary not corporate.
- **Body / secondary**: Light sans-serif where legibility demands it.
- Never cold, never techy.

## Texture Rules
- Never flat colour without texture bleeding through
- Favoured textures: gritty asphalt, woven bamboo/natural material, tapestry/mandala pattern, paper/screen grain
- Grain is part of the voice

## Key Symbols
1. **The Nine of Cups figure** — primary logo/avatar mark. The smiling figure with nine chalices. Do not replace with photography.
2. **The two tin cups on a woven mat** — communion, sharing, campfire. Sub-motif for use in release art and social.
3. **The mandala tapestry** — intricate, global, celebratory. Background/texture source.

## Spatial Language
- Intimate not spectacular
- Perspective is always *among*, never *above*
- Compositions breathe — never crowded
- Frame-within-frame / rectangle-within-square used for printmaking quality

## Mood Words
Contemplative · Communal · Psychedelic without ego · Warm · Textured · Knowing · Unhurried · Layered · Spiritual without dogma · Present · Wellington-rooted · Global in influence

## NOT This Brand
Aggressive · Minimalist · Corporate · Cold · Ironic without feeling · Loudly digital
