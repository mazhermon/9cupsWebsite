Typography

Primary — IM Fell English

Nine of Cups

"Round, unhurried, slightly archaic. Literary, not corporate."

Wordmark, release names, display headings. The brand's literary voice.

Display — Archivo Black

9CUPS

Web/promo. Swiss-inspired, strong edges. Complements the serif.

Body — Inter

Clean, neutral, modern

Used where legibility demands it. Light weight preferred. Secondary text should feel like a caption, not a label. Extreme tracking on secondary lines — event details, dates, platforms.

Rules
  • Large display type: bold weight, oversized scale
  • Extreme tracking on secondary lines
  • Never cold, never techy
  • Secondary text = caption, not label
  • The name commands the frame
